GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE (GSC) — SETUP INSTRUCTIONS
=================================================
Domain: https://pixelmere.site
Sitemap: https://pixelmere.site/sitemap.xml (auto-generated by Next.js)

STEP 1 — ADD THE PROPERTY
  1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
  2. Sign in with the Google account that should own the site
  3. Click "Add property"
  4. Choose "URL prefix" (NOT Domain)
  5. Enter: https://pixelmere.site
  6. Click "Continue"

STEP 2 — VERIFY OWNERSHIP (pick ONE method)
  Method A — HTML tag (easiest):
    1. In GSC choose "HTML tag" as the verification method
    2. Copy the <meta name="google-site-verification" content="..."> tag
    3. Paste it into the site's metadata (ask the AI assistant to add it to
       src/app/layout.tsx, then redeploy)
    4. Click "Verify"
  Method B — DNS TXT record (no redeploy needed):
    1. In GSC choose "DNS" as the verification method
    2. Copy the TXT record value
    3. Add it in your domain registrar / DNS provider (pixelmere.site)
    4. Wait for DNS to propagate (can take a few hours), then "Verify"

STEP 3 — SUBMIT THE SITEMAP
  1. Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" in the left sidebar
  2. In "Add a new sitemap", enter:  sitemap.xml
  3. Click "Submit"
  4. Status should show "Success". If it errors, redeploy first (the sitemap
     only exists after the Next.js site is built/deployed).

STEP 4 — OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED
  - URL Inspection tool: paste https://pixelmere.site, click "Request indexing"
    to get indexed faster.
  - Add https://pixelmere.site to your site's links from Instagram, etc. to
    speed up crawling.
  - Check "Pages" report in a few days for index coverage.
